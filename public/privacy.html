Privacy Policy

Auroa School website — www.auroa.school.nz

Who we are

This website is run by Auroa School, Taranaki, New Zealand. We handle personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

We only collect information that parents, caregivers and students choose to send us through forms on this website, for example:

  • Student Health Profiles for school trips (health, medication, allergy and emergency contact details).
  • Student absence notifications, class list requests, event registrations, lunch orders and hall bookings.
  • Optional notification sign-ups.

Signing in with Google

Before sending a Student Health Profile you are asked to sign in with your Google account. We receive only your Google account's email address and name, which we store with the profile alongside the name you type, so the school knows who sent it. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How we use it

Information is used only by Auroa School staff for the purpose it was given — for example, keeping students safe on trips, recording absences, or organising events. It is not sold, and it is not shared outside the school except where needed to keep a student safe (for example, with medical services in an emergency) or where the law requires it.

Storage and security

Form responses are stored on the school's secure web hosting and can only be viewed by school staff with the website admin login. Information is kept only as long as the school needs it and is then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information the school holds about you or your child, or ask for it to be deleted, by contacting the school office.

Contact

Auroa School office — please use the contact details on the school website.
